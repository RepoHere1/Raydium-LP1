Raydium-LP1

Extreme APR LP scanner

This project scans Raydium pool data, flags APRs at or above your setting (default 999.99%), and applies local safety filters before any future LP automation is considered.

Demo / dry-run build: no wallet keys, no signing, no on-chain LP from this repo.

With the local stack running (scripts\start_stack.cmd or .\scripts\run_dashboard_web.ps1), open the tabbed funnel UI, positions-style table, or stack landing page (served from web\index.html when the dashboard is up).

Double-click START_HERE_SETUP.bat or run .\setup_wizard.ps1
